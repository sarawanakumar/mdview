Links (2.11)

An inline link, one with a title, a reference link, a collapsed reference, and an angle-bracket autolink: https://example.com/angle. Email: mailto:someone@example.com.

Bare web addresses (2.14)

Plain https://example.com/plain and http://example.com/http and www.example.com become links. Trailing punctuation stays outside: https://example.com/end. And in brackets (https://example.com/bracketed), or with a comma, https://example.com/comma, here.

Not in code: https://example.com/in-code stays plain.

Punctuation as written (2.15)

"Straight double quotes", 'straight single quotes', two dashes -- and three ---, and three dots... all stay exactly as typed.
